About The Pastor

CALLED TO AWAKEN A GENERATION

Pastor Samuel Macharia is a passionate herald of the Gospel, author, and lead pastor of Soldout Ministry. He carries a heavy burden for spiritual awakening, biblical truth, winning souls, and raising Kingdom leaders across nations.

His ministry is characterized by dynamic exposition of Scripture and a commitment to helping individuals navigate foundational life choices—including relationships, spiritual discipline, and divine purpose.
